01 — The problem

A form fill says who. It doesn’t say whether they’re worth calling.

An inbound enquiry arrives as a name, an email and a website. Working out whether a dental practice actually needs marketing help — are they running ads, can patients book online, is their Instagram dormant? — meant opening tabs and judging by eye, lead by lead.

02 — The solution

Enrich, analyse, score — automatically.

  • →A HubSpot workflow sends every new form submission to Clay by webhook
  • →Clay enriches services offered, tech stack, Google Ads and online booking
  • →n8n finds the practice’s Instagram and has Gemini analyse its recent posts
  • →Every result is written back to the HubSpot company record
  • →HubSpot scoring rules turn the signals into a fit score and lifecycle stage

Three stages, one company record.

From form submission to a scored, routed lead in HubSpot.

03

Stage 3 · Analyse Instagram — n8n + Gemini
06

Pick up enriched leads.

On a schedule, n8n searches HubSpot for contacts whose enrichment status is complete and loads Vedit’s company profile document. A code step resolves each lead’s business domain — falling back to the email domain and ignoring free providers like Gmail or Hotmail.

07
Agent 1 · Gemini Flash-Lite Serper search tool

Find the Instagram account.

An AI agent runs UK-targeted Google searches (site:instagram.com plus the brand or domain) and verifies the account is the official practice, not a fan page or staff member. No account found? The company is stamped NOT_FOUND in HubSpot and the loop moves on.

08

Scrape and upload the latest 8 posts.

An Apify scraper pulls 20 posts; they’re sorted newest-first and trimmed to 8. Each reel or image is downloaded and sent to the Gemini Files API by resumable upload, then polled every 5 seconds until it’s ready.

09
Agent 2 · Gemini multimodal Structured JSON out

Score the content.

Posting cadence is calculated in code, then a scoring agent reviews the actual media and writes the assessment — broken down below.

10

Update the company in HubSpot.

The parsed analysis fills a dedicated Instagram tab on the company record, and the contact is marked as scored so it isn’t processed twice.

04 — Inside the Instagram scoring

Two AI layers: one watches, one advises.

Hard facts are computed in code, visual judgement comes from Gemini reviewing the real media, and sales advice is written against Vedit’s own service profile. Each layer is told not to go beyond its evidence.

Activity status — computed, not guessed
Active
Last post within 30 days
Slowing
31–90 days · +12 fit points
Dormant
Over 90 days · +12 fit points

A code step works out the average gap between posts, the content mix (reels, images, carousels) and the days since the last post. Average gap alone hides a dead account — eight posts a week apart look healthy even if the last was a year ago — so recency sets the status.

Layer 1 · Vision analysis tool

A direct Gemini API call receives all 8 uploaded files — reels sampled at 2 frames per second — and scores the account as a whole, citing what it actually sees and hears.

Production quality1–10 · lighting, framing, stability, editing, audio
Hook strength1–10 · does the first 1–3 seconds stop the scroll?
Brand consistency1–10 · coherent look, grading, overlays, tone
Content score0–100 composite → Strong, Average or Weak
Layer 2 · Auditor agent

A Gemini agent with Vedit’s company profile in its system prompt must call the vision tool exactly once, pass its scores through unchanged, then write the sales-facing fields itself:

  1. AAreas to improve — tied to the weak scores and naming specific Vedit services.
  2. BContent mix — what the reel/image/carousel split says about strategy.
  3. CPosting cadence — a Dormant account must be called inactive and pitched as a revival opportunity.
Guardrails against made-up scores

Evidence only.

Both prompts forbid inventing techniques, equipment or outcomes that can’t be seen in the media.

Verified tool call.

A code step inspects the agent’s intermediate steps; scores are only written to HubSpot if the vision tool really returned them.

Fail closed.

If analysis fails, every field reads UNAVAILABLE rather than a plausible guess.

Strict schemas.

JSON schemas with enums and ranges on both layers, plus a fixer model to repair malformed output.

07 — In HubSpot

What sales sees.

Company record with the Instagram analysis tab; companies ranked by fit score; the scoring criteria.
Key insight

Each tool does what it’s best at. HubSpot stays the single source of truth.

Clay handles structured enrichment, n8n handles the multi-step AI work Clay can’t, and every result lands on the same HubSpot record — where native scoring, not custom code, decides who gets a call.

08 — Design choices

Built to run unattended.

  1. 01Status fields (pending, checked, scored) stop leads being processed twice.
  2. 02Gemini judges the actual images and reels, not just captions.
  3. 03A wait-and-poll loop capped at 10 attempts handles slow file processing without hanging the run.
  4. 04Scoring lives in HubSpot, so weights can be tuned without touching a workflow.
