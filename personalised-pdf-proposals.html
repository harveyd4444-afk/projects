The personalisation gap.

Even with perfect targeting, most teams see 5–10% reply rates. Top performers break out through personalisation: signal-based emails reach 18%+ replies against 9% for generic templates, and personalised subject lines hit 46% open rates versus 35% — a 31% improvement.

Vedit's specific challenge.

  • Proposals looked generic — no proof of research
  • 30+ minutes of writing per proposal made personalisation impossible at scale
  • Response rates stuck at the 3–5% baseline
  • Lead momentum died between discovery and outreach

The real cost.

When a perfect-fit prospect has already scored 65+, a generic follow-up wastes that qualified lead. Cookie-cutter proposals left an estimated 2–3× of the opportunity on the table.

02 — The solution

One template, copy written per prospect.

  • →Takes qualified lead data: company name, fit score, industry, research
  • →Google Gemini writes personalised copy for each prospect, working from research compared against Vedit’s in-depth company profile document

  • →Creates 4 specific opportunities based on their business model
  • →Merges dynamic copy into the professional HTML template
  • →Converts to PDF and delivers instantly

Research-backed proposals that position Vedit as the expert — not just another agency.

04 — How it works

Six steps, sixty seconds.

From a 65+ qualified lead to a send-ready A4 PDF, with no human writing involved.

01

Trigger from the Lead Qualification Engine.

When a lead scores 65+, it triggers the proposal generator.

02

Pull client research data.

  • Company name, industry, fit score
  • Company description carried over from Workflow 1
  • Recommended services
03

Download the HTML template.

Fetches the professional proposal template from Google Drive — Vedit branding, layout and design already set.

04
AI agent · Gemini Char limits enforced

Write personalised copy.

  • Reads client research plus industry context, checked against Vedit’s in-depth company profile document so recommendations stay accurate to what Vedit actually offers
  • Writes 3 personalised service cards tailored to the business
  • Writes 4 specific opportunities unique to the industry
  • Generates the hero headline and call to action
  • Structured JSON output — restaurants get food videography, e-commerce gets video ads
05

JavaScript template merge.

  • Inserts the AI copy into the HTML template, replacing tags such as CLIENT_NAME and CARD_1_TITLE
  • Validates that every placeholder is filled
  • Returns clean HTML
06

Convert to PDF and deliver.

  • Generates a professional one-page A4 PDF
  • Filenamed “Proposal — [Company Name].pdf”
  • Ready to send, 60 seconds end to end

Key insight

Speed × personalisation.

Sales used to choose between generic templates — fast, low conversion — and custom-written proposals: slow, high conversion. Now every prospect gets a fast custom-written proposal that names their company, references their industry and recommends relevant services.

Personalised proposals are the difference between a 5% and a 15%+ response rate. Character limits keep every proposal inside one A4 page, so nothing overflows and nothing needs checking.
