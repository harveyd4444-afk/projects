01 — The problem

Twenty minutes of scrolling per prospect.

Before any outreach, Vedit needed to understand each prospect's Instagram strategy — and every step was manual:

  • Finding the right account, avoiding fakes and personal profiles
  • Scrolling posts to judge content quality
  • Reading engagement, posting patterns and content gaps
  • Noting by hand what needed improvement

Without that research, outreach felt generic and missed industry-specific opportunities.

02 — The solution

Discovery and analysis, both automated.

  • →Searches for the official Instagram profile via Serper, verified by AI
  • →Scrapes the 8 most recent posts with the Apify Instagram scraper
  • →Analyses those posts with Google Gemini
  • →Generates a structured brief with gaps and recommendations, measured against Vedit’s in-depth company profile document
  • →Returns the data to Google Sheets in three minutes

05 — Worked example

A restaurant profile, analysed.

What the analysis found
  • Strong food photography, but posting is inconsistent — gaps of three to four weeks
  • No behind-the-scenes or kitchen video content at all
Brief output

“Food photos perform well but inconsistent posting loses momentum. Opportunity: weekly kitchen videos plus three posts a week would grow followers and drive orders.”

Key insight

Three minutes of research replaces twenty minutes of manual work.

The brief informs the proposal generator, which then recommends specific services against the gaps actually found on the account. Every piece of outreach is research-backed rather than generic.
