01 — The problem

Lead lists don't show hiring intent.

Finding companies with real budget is the hard part. Vedit needed to target:

  • Companies actively hiring, which demonstrates spending
  • Specific roles — Social Media Manager, Content Creator, Head of Marketing
  • Companies with actual budget, not early-stage startups

Traditional lead lists show none of that. LinkedIn job postings show all of it.

02 — The solution

Hiring signal in, enriched lead out.

  • →Searches LinkedIn for specific job titles
  • →AI checks whether the company is a good fit for Vedit, comparing the research against an in-depth company profile document
  • →Pulls their website and analyses the business
  • →Finds verified contact details through Apollo
  • →Saves only the leads with confirmed contact data

High-intent leads only. If they're hiring, they have budget.

Five steps from posting to contact.

03
Gemini Company profile doc Skip if no match

AI fit check.

Is this hiring company a good fit for Vedit? The research is compared against Vedit's in-depth company profile document — services, positioning, ideal client — so the fit score is accurate rather than a guess. Anything that doesn't match is dropped before enrichment spends a credit.

Key insight

A job posting is proof the budget exists.

When a company advertises for a Social Media Manager or Content Creator, it has already committed spend to that function. It either hires in-house or comes back to outsourcing later. Either way, the money is there — which is exactly the signal a cold lead list can't give you.
