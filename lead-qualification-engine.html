01 — The problem

Spray-and-pray outreach was burning the week.

Vedit Media needed to move beyond spray-and-pray cold outreach. Manual prospecting was inefficient, time-consuming, and hit-or-miss. They needed a way to systematically find companies that actually matched their ICP — before any outreach happened.

02 — The solution

An n8n pipeline that qualifies before it hands over.

  • →Daily automated queries to the Companies House API (last 24 hours only)
  • →Three-stage AI filtering so only high-fit companies pass through
  • →Website verification → company research → fit evaluation, scored against Vedit’s in-depth company profile document
  • →Multi-gate quality control on confidence and fit thresholds
  • →Only qualified leads reach the sales team for targeted outreach

05 — How it works

Nine steps, no human in the loop.

The full pipeline from trigger to stored prospect, running unattended every 24 hours.

01

Daily trigger at 8 AM.

Workflow runs automatically every 24 hours.

02

Query Companies House API.

Fetch newly registered Manchester companies from the last 24 hours.

03

Split companies into records.

Each company is processed individually through the pipeline.

04

Basic filter — active status.

Pre-filter for active companies only.

05
Agent 1 · Gemini Gate: confidence 80+

Website verification.

  • Uses Serper Search to find the official website
  • Verifies it exists and is legitimate
  • Scores confidence 0–100
  • Excludes Vedit's own site
06
Agent 2 · Gemini Structured JSON out

Company research.

  • Analyses the company's official website
  • Extracts industry, products, services, target audience, business model
  • Captures social profiles, content activity, marketing approach
  • Confidence scoring 0–100 with reasoning and sources
07
Agent 3 · Gemini fit_score 0–100 Company profile doc

Fit evaluation.

  • Compares the research against Vedit’s in-depth company profile document — services, positioning, ideal client — for an accurate fit score
  • Analyses ICP match, relevance and opportunity
  • Generates the fit score used by the quality gate
  • Recommends the relevant Vedit services
08

Quality gates & deduplication.

  • Fit score 65+ only
  • Confidence checks on website and research stages
  • Duplicate detection against the Prospects sheet
  • Failures routed to a manual review bucket
09

Save qualified prospect.

  • New qualified leads appended to Google Sheets
  • Duplicates discarded automatically
  • Dashboard updates in real time
06 — Daily cycle

Every 24 hours.

Discover

Companies House queried for new registrations in the last 24 hours — ~25 new companies found and verified.

Analyse

Three-stage AI analysis applied: website verification, company research, fit evaluation.

Filter

Quality gates drop low-confidence and low-fit leads before anything reaches the team.

Deliver

Results flow to Google Sheets, the dashboard refreshes, and sales sees new qualified leads instantly.

Key insight

A 20% qualification rate is the proof the algorithm works.

Not every lead scores high — that is what prevents false positives — but quality leads emerge consistently. Daily runs keep the sales team stocked with fresh prospects, and the multi-stage approach means only high-confidence leads ever reach a human.
